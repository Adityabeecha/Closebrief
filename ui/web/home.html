Every figure, traced before you read it.

Numbers and words are made by different things. That separation is the product.

  1. Numbers firstEvery KPI is computed in code from your data before any text is written.
  2. Words secondThe model receives those figures already formatted and is told to copy them exactly.
  3. Then a checkEach number in the draft must match a computed fact or your own context notes. Rounding is fine; invention isn't.
  4. You approveNothing leaves as final until a person signs it off.

Built around the close you already run.

  1. ImportCSV or Excel from NetSuite, QuickBooks or spreadsheets, any layout; budgets joined from a second file.
  2. ComputeValue, month-over-month, year-over-year, budget variance, trends and anomalies, in code.
  3. DraftPlain-English commentary per KPI, citing the context notes you keep.
  4. CheckEvery figure in the prose traced to a computed fact or flagged.
  5. ReviewWork the queue from exceptions down, edit, approve.Your part
  6. ShareExecutive digest, board pack, PowerPoint, Slack or email on a schedule.
